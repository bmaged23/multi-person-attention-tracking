## Exact footage

The two excerpts use **IS1004d.L_orig.avi** and **IS1004d.R_orig.avi**, 60–72
seconds from the synchronized left and right meeting cameras. Each retains its
native 720×576 resolution and 25 FPS. The combined demo shows the left view,
then the right view. Track IDs restart for the second clip and are session-local
spatial labels, not identities matched between cameras.

## Modifications

Trimmed to 12 seconds per camera, removed audio, re-encoded as H.264, added
face bounding boxes and heuristic attention labels, concatenated the annotated
clips, and created a looping GIF at 8 FPS. The demo makes no claim about the
participants' actual cognitive attention. The source authors do not endorse
this application or its predictions.

The MP4 retains source resolution and 25 FPS. The GIF retains source resolution
but reduces frame rate and uses an indexed palette for README compatibility.
Source recordings are not committed; only the credited derivative demo is.

The older CDED-7 classroom footage remains local and is not used in public media.
